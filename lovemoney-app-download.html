LoveMoney App Download: Safe Android & iOS Options

Looking for the official LoveMoney app? Learn how to safely set up mobile browser play, check Android APK risks, play on iOS, and avoid fake App Store scams.

Is There an Official LoveMoney App?

If you are actively searching the Google Play Store or Apple App Store for the LoveMoney app, you will not find it. Because LoveMoney (developed by Buwu) is an independent indie game featuring mature (18+) themes, it inherently violates the strict content policies of official app distribution platforms.

Security Warning: Any applications available on standard app stores claiming to be the "Official LoveMoney Game" are generally fake counterparts, wallpapers, or malicious software designed to scam players. Do not install them.

Because Apple strictly prohibits third-party app installations (sideloading) without jailbreaking, there is no playable iOS IPA app file for LoveMoney. Fortunately, iPhone users do not need an app. You can play the full, uncensored game utilizing the Safari browser directly. The Best Alternative: "Add to Home Screen" Method

Since LoveMoney is built using modern HTML5 technology, the absolute safest and fastest way to get the "App Experience" on any phone is by playing it through your mobile browser and creating a direct shortcut. This method requires zero downloads and consumes no storage space:

  1. Open the official game link or our Where to Play Page using Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS).
  2. Tap your browser's menu button (the three dots in Chrome, or the Share button in Safari).
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm the name. You now have a LoveMoney "App" icon right on your phone!

When you launch the game from this newly created icon, it will open in full-screen mode, completely hiding the browser interface so it acts and feels exactly like a real, downloaded application.
