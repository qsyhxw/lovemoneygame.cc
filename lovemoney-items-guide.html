Buying strategy without overclaiming

1 · Start with Feather

The public descriptions that include a number agree on the $100 Feather and its $2-per-click result, making it the clearest early efficiency purchase.

2 · Watch the shop

Use the shop's current availability signal and record the price/effect shown in your build. Do not import a later-item value from a different version.

3 · Decide before late items

Public guides connect late or Soap-related purchases with route changes, but the exact map is not official. Save or note your choices if you are comparing outcomes.

What this page does not replace

This is an items reference, not a full walkthrough or an all-endings chapter. For route summaries, use the All Endings guide; for basic controls and progression, use How to Play.

OLD vs UPDATE: check your build

The official Buwu listing labels OLD as an older version with bugs, unfinished features, and translation issues. It describes UPDATE as adding mechanics, secret content, and a new story, but it does not say whether the shop list, item values, or route triggers changed. That is why this guide avoids a fixed “complete list” claim for every build.

Before buying

Check the item name, displayed cost, and any visible effect in the shop. Treat old screenshots and guide tables as version-specific evidence.

When comparing endings

Record the version label and purchase choices. The public material does not justify a universal rule that one item alone always forces one ending.

Items FAQ

Which LoveMoney item has a confirmed price and effect?

The Feather is the only item currently supported by multiple public gameplay descriptions with the same price and numeric effect: $100 and $2 per click. Buwu's itch.io listing does not publish a complete item table.

What are the price and effect of the Brush or Soap?

Public reports disagree or omit exact values for later items. This guide does not present a Brush or Soap price, multiplier, or unlock threshold as confirmed; check the item shown in your game version.

Does buying Soap guarantee a specific ending?

Public guides associate late or Soap-related purchases with different route outcomes, but the official listing does not publish a complete item-to-ending map. Treat the relationship as version-sensitive rather than a guaranteed universal rule. See the All Endings guide for the separate route overview.

Do OLD and UPDATE versions have the same items?

The official listing says UPDATE contains additional mechanics, secret content, and a new story, but it does not enumerate shop changes. Check the version you are playing before relying on a later-item price or effect.
