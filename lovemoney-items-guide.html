Complete LoveMoney Items Guide: Locations & Unlocks

In LoveMoney, the items you unlock are more than just idle-game upgrades. Each item you purchase directly impacts Harvey Harvington's state of mind and determines which ending you will receive. Here is the comprehensive guide to every item, its effects, and where to find it.

Introduction to the Item System

The core gameplay loop of LoveMoney involves clicking to earn money towards your $25,000 medical goal. To speed up this process, you can purchase upgrades in the form of Items. However, the shop is simultaneously a moral test. The game tracks not just how much money you have, but exactly what you bought.

  • Economic Effect: Increases passive income and click value.
  • Narrative Effect: Changes Harvey's dialogue and on-screen sprite animations.

Consumables & Hidden Items

Currently, Lovemoney functions heavily as a linear tracker; there are no hidden mini-game drops apart from the primary upgrade shop string. Every narrative-altering item is explicitly visible in the main shop UI. However, tracking your item inventory limit is crucial when interacting with the Moral Choices system.

FAQ: How Items Affect Endings

Should I buy every item to win easily?

No! While buying the Lipstick and Soap will make reaching $25,000 much faster, it acts as a betrayal of Harvey's trust. If you want the "Pure Heart/Savior" ending, you must pacing your clicks and completely ignore the final, invasive items in the shop.

Can I sell or refund items?

Once an item is purchased and used on Harvey, the narrative consequence is permanent for that run. We highly recommend saving your game manually before buying the Lipstick or Soap so you can reload and explore different paths.
