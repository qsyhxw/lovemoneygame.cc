What triggers the worst ending?

The safest summary is that some early items may still allow a positive route, while Soap is the major branch to avoid for the good route. The main guide has the full caveats.

Yes. LoveMoney is inspired by BLOODMONEY! and features the same character, Harvey. See Harvey Harvington Wiki for more context.
