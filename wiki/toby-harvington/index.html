Toby Harvington — Harvey's Son Character Wiki & Lore Guide

Toby Harvington is the young son of Harvey Harvington and Eun-Mi Harvington in BLOODMONEY! by SHROOMYCHRIST. Although a non-playable character, Toby represents innocence at the heart of one of indie gaming's most tragic family narratives.

Who Is Toby Harvington?

Toby Harvington is the young son of Harvey Harvington (age 32) and Eun-Mi Harvington (age 34) in the BLOODMONEY! game series created by developer SHROOMYCHRIST. While Toby never appears as a playable or directly interactive character, his existence is a crucial narrative thread that adds emotional weight to the entire Harvington family tragedy.

As the innocent child caught between his parents' increasingly dark circumstances, Toby serves as a powerful symbol of what's at stake in the games' moral dilemmas. Harvey's desire to provide for Toby and Eun-Mi drives much of his willingness to endure suffering, while Eun-Mi's need to support her son becomes a justification for her morally questionable decisions in the sequel.

Toby's exact age has never been officially confirmed by SHROOMYCHRIST, though he is consistently depicted as a young child. According to community-maintained wikis, his birthday is listed as May 18th, although this detail has not been verified through official developer statements.

Toby's Role in the Games

🩸 BLOODMONEY!

In the original BLOODMONEY!, Toby is part of Harvey's background lore. Harvey's motivation to endure pain at his roadside booth is tied to his family's financial struggles—he needs money to support Eun-Mi and Toby. Players learn about Toby through Harvey's occasional dialogue references to his family.

🖥️ Human Expenditure Program (HEP)

In the sequel (BLOODMONEY 2), Toby's role gains significantly more narrative importance. Eun-Mi uses the need to provide for Toby as one of her primary justifications for trapping Harvey's consciousness inside a digital simulation. The mounting debts and the responsibility of raising a child alone push Eun-Mi toward increasingly unethical decisions.

💕 LoveMoney (Fan Game by Buwu)

In LoveMoney, Toby does not appear directly but remains part of Harvey's canonical family background. Harvey's guilt about his wife and son is a recurring emotional element—particularly when the player pushes Harvey toward intimate interactions. Developer Buwu specifically chose to keep Harvey's family intact as part of the narrative, ensuring that the theme of infidelity carries genuine moral weight.

Toby's Fate Across Endings

How player choices in BLOODMONEY! affect the Harvington family

💚 Good Ending

The Harvington family remains intact. Harvey earns the money through minimal harm, preserving his relationships. Toby continues to grow up in a functional (if imperfect) household. This represents the best outcome for the child.

⚖️ Neutral Ending

Harvey becomes hardened and paranoid from his trauma. According to community lore sources, he becomes overprotective of Toby, turning his parental instincts into controlling behavior. His relationship with Eun-Mi deteriorates, and he begins drinking heavily—creating a tense home environment for Toby.

💀 Bad Ending (Gun)

Harvey dies after the player purchases and uses the gun. Toby loses his father. The full impact on Toby is left to the player's imagination, making this one of the most emotionally devastating outcomes for those who consider the family's perspective.

🔓 True Ending (HEP)

In BLOODMONEY 2's true ending, Harvey discovers the truth about his digital imprisonment and ultimately terminates his own program. According to multiple community sources, Toby remains unaware of the full tragedy that destroyed his family—particularly the extent of his mother's actions and his father's suffering.

Harvington Family Dynamics

Harvey & Toby: Father and Son

Harvey's relationship with Toby is defined by deep paternal love and protectiveness. As a stay-at-home parent and primary cook in the household, Harvey handles most of Toby's daily care. His willingness to endure any form of suffering—whether physical pain in BLOODMONEY! or moral compromise in LoveMoney—stems partly from his desperation to provide for his son.

In the Neutral Ending, this protective instinct transforms into something unhealthy. Harvey's trauma manifests as controlling, overbearing parenting toward Toby, suggesting that even in endings where Harvey survives, the damage extends beyond him to affect his child.

Eun-Mi & Toby: Mother's Justification

Eun-Mi Harvington's relationship with Toby takes on a darker dimension in BLOODMONEY 2 (HEP). The need to support her son becomes one of her primary rationalizations for trapping Harvey in the Human Expenditure Program. With mounting family debts, Eun-Mi convinces herself that exploiting Harvey's digital consciousness for streaming revenue is a necessary sacrifice for Toby's wellbeing.

This creates one of the game's most uncomfortable moral layers: a mother committing arguably monstrous acts while motivated—at least in part—by genuine concern for her child. The game deliberately avoids presenting a simple hero–villain dynamic, using Toby's needs as the emotional wedge that makes Eun-Mi's choices understandable even as they remain indefensible.

The Tragic Core

Toby Harvington is arguably the most tragic figure in the BLOODMONEY! universe—not because of what happens to him directly, but because of what he represents. He is the innocent bystander in a family torn apart by economic desperation, exploitation, and moral collapse. No matter which ending the player reaches, Toby's childhood has been irrevocably shaped by events he has no control over and may never fully understand.

Community Discussions & Fan Theories

💭 Does Toby Know the Truth?

One of the most debated questions in the BLOODMONEY! fandom is whether Toby ever discovers what happened to his father. In the True Ending, community consensus suggests Toby remains unaware. However, fan theories explore scenarios where an older Toby uncovers the truth—potentially setting up narrative possibilities for future entries in the series.

🔮 Toby's Future

Fans frequently speculate about what happens to Toby after the events of the games. Common theories include: Toby growing up to become a programmer like his mother, Toby discovering archived evidence of his father's digital prison, or Toby being the emotional catalyst for a potential BLOODMONEY 3. None of these theories have been confirmed or denied by SHROOMYCHRIST.

❤️ Community Sympathy

Within the BLOODMONEY! community, Toby consistently ranks among the most sympathized-with characters. Players express protective feelings toward him, with many citing Toby's unknowing innocence as the detail that makes the Harvington family tragedy most emotionally impactful. Fan art and fan fiction frequently explore "what-if" scenarios where the family finds healing.
