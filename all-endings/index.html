What the Soap decision tells you

Soap is one of the most frequently mentioned branch markers in player discussions, which makes it useful when comparing route notes. It should not be treated as a fully documented universal switch: different versions and community playthroughs report different surrounding conditions.

Use it as a route checkpoint

Record whether the Soap choice appears in your run and which version you are playing before comparing an outcome with another guide.

Do not infer a guaranteed ending

The official listing does not state that one Soap choice alone determines every ending. Exact consequences remain a reported variation.
