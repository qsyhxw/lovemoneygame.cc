Best Websites for Games Like LoveMoney

Once players finish the emotionally draining, multi-ending experience of the LoveMoney visual novel, the immediate next question is: "What other websites host games exactly like this?" Because independent, 18+ psychological thrillers aren't allowed on mainstream stores like Apple's App Store—and struggle against Steam's strict content curation—you need to know the right alternative platforms to look at.

1. itch.io (The Official Home)

If you are looking for websites like the one that gave birth to LoveMoney, itch.io should be your absolute first stop. This is where Buwu (the developer) officially uploads their games.

  • Why it's great: It has heavily curated "Psychological Horror" and "Adult Visual Novel" tags.
  • Monetization: Like LoveMoney, many games here are "Name Your Own Price" (effectively free).
  • Safety: Highest safety rating for downloading indie game ZIP files compared to pirate sites.

2. F95Zone (The Community Hub)

When gamers ask about adult gaming websites, F95Zone is usually the unspoken titan. It is an active forum dedicated entirely to the development, discussion, and translation of adult games and visual novels.

  • Why it's great: Direct communication with developers and comprehensive walk-throughs created by hardcore fans.
  • Discoverability: It's significantly easier to find niche, heavy-theme games here than searching blindly on Google.

Warning: F95Zone is an unmoderated 18+ forum intended strictly for adult users.

3. Patreon (Support the Creators)

While not a traditional "gaming storefront", many creators who make intense, choice-driven 18+ games rely on Patreon funding runs to afford their engines and external artwork. If you loved the indie spirit of LoveMoney, subscribing to visual novel creators on Patreon is the best way to get Early Access versions of games before they hit the public web.
