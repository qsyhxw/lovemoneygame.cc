LoveMoney Gameplay Overview: What You Need to Know

Before you download or play LoveMoney online, get a complete understanding of its unique mechanics. Far from a standard visual novel, LoveMoney combines clicker progression, deep moral dilemmas, and intense psychological relationship management.

Basic Gameplay Loop

The central objective of LoveMoney is straightforward: You must raise $25,000 to pay for a life-saving medical procedure. The gameplay loop works as follows:

  1. Clicking: You interact directly with the character on-screen (Harvey Harvington) to generate passive and active income.
  2. Upgrading: As you earn cash, you can visit the shop to buy items that radically increase your earning multiplier.
  3. Reacting: The items you purchase change how you interact with Harvey, triggering new dialogue, distress meters, and shifting the narrative.

Key Game Mechanics

Branching Dialogues & Moral Choices

Unlike traditional visual novels where you pick A or B from a text box, your "choices" in LoveMoney are determined by what you do to Harvey to extract money. Will you progress slowly using gentle tools? Or will you force progress by purchasing invasive items? Your restraint acts as the primary moral compass of the game.

If you want to understand exactly how each action tips the scales between the "Good" and "Bad" endings, check out our deep dive in the Moral Choices Guide.

Resource & Item Management

Every item in the game is a double-edged sword. Buying the Lipstick or the Soap will grant you an immense monetary boost, making the $25,000 goal trivial to hit. However, these items cause lasting psychological trauma to the protagonist and permanently lock you into the game's darkest endings.

Censored vs Uncensored Mode Differences

Because LoveMoney tackles extreme mature themes (18+), there are distinct differences between the public-safe versions and the developer's intended release:

  • Uncensored Version (Recommended): Contains zero pixelation or blurring. The artwork, animations, and narrative text present the adult themes in their entirety. This version is exclusively available through the official desktop download route; Android APK searchers should read the APK safety guide first.
  • Censored Version: Mostly found on restricted web portals. It obscures explicit imagery and sometimes cuts dialogue lines to comply with terms of service.
