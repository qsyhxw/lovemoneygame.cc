LoveMoney Game Run Paths: Optimal Routing Guide

Because LoveMoney relies heavily on repetition and moral decision-making, planning your run path is essential for unlocking all verified endings efficiently. Whether you are aiming for the emotional Love ending or trying to achieve a sub-30 minute Speedrun to secure $25,000, following an optimized path through the game's shop and dialogue tree saves you from endless clicking frustration.

1. The "Pure Money" Run Path

This path is entirely focused on hitting the massive $25,000 goal as ruthlessly as possible, completely ignoring Harvey's deteriorating mental state.

  • Early Game Upgrade Priority: Immediately rush passive income items in the shop. Do not waste clicking stamina on dialogue options unless forced.
  • Crucial Decision - The Soap Item: You must purchase the Soap early to unlock aggressive multiplier scaling, even though it heavily penalizes your relationship meter.
  • Dialogue Focus: Always select the options that extract the maximum monetary value from Harvey, bypassing any emotional checking or reassurance.

See our full Money Farming Guide for exact click-to-dollar math optimization.

2. The "Love / Redemption" Run Path

This route sacrifices rapid economic gain to focus on nurturing your relationship with Harvey, leading to the game's more empathetic conclusions.

  • Pacing: Do not blitz through the clicking interface. Allow stamina to recover slowly, preventing Harvey's stress meter from maxing out.
  • Item Economy: Purchase the Feather and other gentle interaction items. Avoid abrasive interactions entirely.
  • Dialogue Focus: Prioritize understanding the 12 hidden lore notes and exploring Harvey's medical debt trauma gently.
