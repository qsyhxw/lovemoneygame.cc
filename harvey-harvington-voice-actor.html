Who is the Harvey Harvington Voice Actor? (LoveMoney VA REVEALED)

Wondering who provides the iconic voice for Harvey Harvington? Discover the actor behind the character and learn about the game's unique voice acting features.

Quick Facts Reveal

Character Harvey Harvington
Voice Actor (VA) Dexter Manning
Voice Type Partial (Reactions / Grunts)

1. The Voice Actor Revealed: Dexter Manning

If you've spent any time interacting with the charming (and sometimes distressed) Harvey Harvington in LoveMoney or its stylistic predecessor BloodMoney, you’ve likely noticed his distinct voice. The gasps, the low chuckles, and the dialogue cues add massive depth to the game's psychological tension.

The official voice actor (VA) for Harvey Harvington across the game's universe is definitively Dexter Manning.

Dexter Manning is well-known in the indie gaming and animation internet spheres, frequently lending his versatile voice to quirky, darker, or highly emotional characters. His portrayal of Harvey is widely praised by the community for perfectly capturing the character’s blend of desperate optimism, vulnerability, and eventually, profound distress depending on your gameplay choices. You can follow Dexter's other projects and video uploads on his official YouTube channel and social platforms.

2. Is the Game Fully Voice Acted?

One common question players searching for the VA have is regarding the sheer amount of dialogue present in the game files. LoveMoney utilizes what is known in the indie industry as Partial Voice Acting.

  • What this means: The game does not feature a fully dubbed audio track for every single line of written text like a high-budget AAA visual novel.
  • How it works: Instead, Dexter Manning provided a robust library of reactionary audio clips—grunts, sighs, chuckles, short catchphrases, and pain indicators. When you click Harvey or use an item on him (like a Feather or a Brush), these specific voice clips are triggered to highlight his emotional state, significantly boosting the immersion without requiring thousands of lines of recorded dialogue.

3. The Connection Between Harvey, BloodMoney, and LoveMoney

Whether you are exploring the darker origins of BloodMoney or the somewhat sweeter, fan-driven interactions of LoveMoney, Harvey is fundamentally the same character template. The iconic voice performance by Dexter Manning serves as the anchor that bridges these different timelines and interactive versions together, ensuring that Harvey always retains his signature anxious yet lovable personality regardless of the mod or patch you are playing.
