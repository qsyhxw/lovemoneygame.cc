LoveMoney 2: Release Date, Sequel News & Everything We Know

For fans who have unlocked all endings in the original 18+ visual novel, the most common question is: will there be a LoveMoney 2? The short answer is yes, but the details require some clarification so you don't fall victim to fake download scams.

Want to know when LoveMoney 2 drops?

Enter your email to get notified the second Buwu announces a demo, early access, or an official release date for the sequel.

Is There Going to Be a LoveMoney 2?

Yes! Developer Buwu has officially confirmed that a sequel to LoveMoney is currently in development. On January 14, 2026, the creator announced progress on the next chapter of the game, which currently operates under the working title Love Together.

While LoveMoney was originally created as a fan-game tribute to the horror clicker BLOODMONEY!, its massive popularity on itch.io has prompted the developer to expand the universe into a full-fledged standalone sequel.

When is the LoveMoney 2 Release Date?

As of April 2026, there is no finalized release date for Love Together (LoveMoney 2). The developer is known for releasing periodic updates (such as the LoveMoney PERFECT edition released previously), but a full sequel requires significant development time for new art assets, branching narratives, and scenes.

Where to check for updates:

The only reliable source for release news is Buwu's official itch.io page. We will update this guide as soon as a concrete date or demo is announced.

Beware of Fake "LoveMoney 2 APK" Scams

⚠️ Fake Download Warning

Because the sequel is highly anticipated, many malicious websites have started advertising fake LoveMoney 2 Mobile APK or LoveMoney 2 Early Access Download files.

  • The game is not available for download yet.
  • There will likely not be a native Android or iOS app at launch (just like the first game, refer to our mobile playing guide).
  • Any site claiming to have a leaked download of the sequel is distributing malware.
