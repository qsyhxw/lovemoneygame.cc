LoveMoney English Version: Download Guide & Russian Menu Fix

Because LoveMoney was originally created by an independent Russian developer, many international players get confused when looking for the English version. Even after downloading the English file, you might be surprised to find the game's menu settings are still in Cyrillic.

This comprehensive guide explains how to get the correct English build, why the menus are untranslated, and provides a simple translation cheat sheet to help you navigate the UI.

How to Download the English Version

Unlike many modern games where you select your language inside the client or settings menu, LoveMoney offers its language versions as totally separate downloads on the official itch.io page. You cannot change the language from within the Russian version of the game.

To ensure you have the English dialogue and story scenes, you must download the files labeled with "eng". The developer strongly recommends ignoring the "OLD" files due to severe translation issues and bugs.

Why is the Menu Still in Russian? (Known Issue)

💡 You didn't download the wrong version!

If you launch your newly extracted LoveMoney_eng_UPDATE and notice the startup or ESC menus are completely in Russian, do not panic. The developer confirmed in the official itch.io comments that the core system UI (saving, loading, volume sliders) was left untranslated in the English build.

However, the core story, your interactions with Harvey, the collectible notes, and the shop items are fully translated into English.

Quick Translation for the Russian Menu

Because the game is incredibly short—most of the endings can be achieved in a single 30-minute session—you will rarely need to save or dig into the system settings. However, if you need to pause or tweak the audio, here is a quick translation cheat sheet for the main UI options:

Tip: If you need to change settings in-depth, we highly recommend using the camera feature on the Google Translate mobile app to translate the screen in real-time.

English Translation Quality

The original "OLD" version of the game suffered from very clunky, machine-translation-style writing. This made the complex themes of debt manipulation and psychological stress hard to follow, leading to confusion among English-speaking players regarding the differences from BloodMoney.

Fortunately, the UPDATE version significantly improved the English localizations. It is highly readable and effectively conveys Harvey's deteriorating mental state and the subtle dialogue nuances required to make the correct ethical choices.
