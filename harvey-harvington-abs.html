Harvey Harvington Abs: Official Sprites vs Fanart Lore

Does Harvey really take off his shirt in a secret ending? We uncover the truth about his official design, Tumblr Sexyman fame, and fan-made art.

As of the PERFECT 2026 update, his design remains fully clothed. If future DLCs introduce a beach episode, we will update this guide immediately.

❓ Does Harvey ever take off his shirt in the core game?

No. There are absolutely no official sprites in LoveMoney or BloodMoney where Harvey is shirtless or revealing his abs. All such images are 100% community fanart.

1. The Truth: Are There Official Shredded Sprites?

If you’ve been browsing TikTok edits, Twitter fan threads, or Pinterest boards dedicated to LoveMoney, you have likely stumbled across artwork depicting the well-dressed Harvey Harvington showing off a rather impressive set of abs. This sparked a massive surge in players investigating the game, asking one specific question: Is there a secret shirtless ending, or is the "Harvey Harvington abs" sprite completely fan-made?

Let’s cut straight to the chase to save you hours of furiously clicking trying to unlock a non-existent route: In the official game files created by developer Buwu, Harvey’s character design is strictly formal.

Throughout every single ending—whether you play the gentle route or the most punishing one—he exclusively wears his signature attire: a slightly disheveled suit jacket, a crisp white button-up shirt, and a pink bow tie. There is no hidden "clothing damage" modifier, "shirtless" item in the shop menu, or secret cheat code to undress him.

2. Where Did the "Abs" Trend Come From?

If it's not in the game, why are so many people searching for it? This phenomenon is purely the result of the internet's powerful "Tumblr Sexyman" effect intersecting with vibrant fan-fiction communities.

Because Harvey’s character archetype revolves around a desperate, sharply-dressed man subjected to the player’s whims, he garnered a massive, passionate fanbase quickly. Fan artists took creative liberties with his hidden physique. The headcanon that beneath his neat, modest white shirt lies a chiseled, muscular build became so widespread in the fanart community (often appearing on body pillows / dakimakura mockups) that new players mistook it for official, datamined content.

3. Looking for Real Mature Content?

While you won't find a ripped, shirtless Harvey in the game’s core assets, LoveMoney is still an incredibly mature game equipped with intense psychological themes, suggestive dialogue, and heavy R18 connotations depending on which patch you are playing.
