5 Best Visual Novel Games Like LoveMoney

Finished LoveMoney and craving more moral dilemmas, psychological twists, and clicker-hybrid mechanics? Check out our top recommendations for the best alternative visual novels that scratch the same itch.

Why LoveMoney Defines This Thriller Genre

LoveMoney isn't just a standard dating sim or visual novel. It blends a passive clicker/progression system (earning $25,000 for surgery) with deep moral branches. Every item you buy or choice you make dynamically alters your relationship with the protagonist. The games below mirror this dark, psychological format where your choices carry heavy consequences.

Top Recommendations

1. Slay the Princess

Overview: A highly acclaimed psychological horror visual novel where you are given one simple task: Slay the Princess. Like LoveMoney, the story is entirely shaped by your choices, leading to multiple wildly different, branching paths.

Why it's like LoveMoney: Every action and dialogue choice fractures your psyche and changes the very nature of the Princess. If you enjoyed the psychological pressure and unpredictability of LoveMoney's branching narrative, this is a must-play.

2. Needy Streamer Overload

Overview: You play as "P-chan," the producer managing a young streamer's career over 30 days. You must balance her Stress, Affection, and Mental Darkness to hit follower milestones without pushing her over the edge.

Why it's like LoveMoney: Both games are built around interacting with a single character in a confined interface while balancing a specific metric (followers vs. money) against the character's mental health and moral boundaries. Both lead to very dark alternative endings based on "bad" resource management.

3. BloodMoney

Overview: We can't talk about LoveMoney without mentioning its main inspiration. BloodMoney is the original game that laid the thematic groundwork for LoveMoney's clicker-visual novel mechanics.

Why play it: If you loved the core mechanics of Lovemoney, playing the original is essential. For a deep dive into the differences, check out our strict comparison guide: LoveMoney vs BloodMoney. If you want mature content variations, check the BloodMoney 18+ Guide.

4. John Doe

Overview: A short, free, and incredibly atmospheric surreal indie visual novel available on itch.io revolving around a relentless admirer.

Why it's like LoveMoney: Both thrive in the indie itch.io space and excel at making players uncomfortable through boundary-pushing themes and slightly unhinged interactions with a specific character.

5. Doki Doki Literature Club!

Overview: The legendary visual novel that appears to be a cute dating simulator but quickly spirals into heavy psychological horror.

Why it's like LoveMoney: It sets the gold standard for games that mislead the player with innocent aesthetics before transitioning into unsettling emotional or moral horror.

Are there other mobile alternatives?

Finding intense psychological thrillers optimized for mobile can be difficult, as many are built exclusively for PC on Steam. However, the indie sphere—specifically developers on itch.io—often provide APKs. If you are specifically looking for Android-compatible variants of these dark visual novels, BloodMoney currently offers similar robust mobile support just like LoveMoney.
