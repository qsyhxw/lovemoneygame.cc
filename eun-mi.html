Who Is Eun-Mi Harvington?

As Harvey Harvington's Wife, Eun-Mi Harvington serves as a central and complex figure in the BLOODMONEY! universe created by SHROOMYCHRIST. While she doesn't appear directly in the original BLOODMONEY! game, she is frequently referenced in the lore and plays a crucial role in understanding her husband's tragic backstory and motivations.

In the sequel game Human Expenditure Program (HEP), Eun-Mi becomes a playable character. Players take on her perspective as she exerts control over Harvey, providing a dark look into their troubled marriage and the psychological dynamics at play.

Her character represents the complex themes of the BLOODMONEY! universe: obsession with money, deteriorating relationships, and the moral compromises people make when facing financial hardship.

Relationship with Harvey Harvington

According to BLOODMONEY! lore, Harvey put significant effort into his 10-year relationship with Eun-Mi. However, after he lost his job as an investment banker, their marriage began to fall apart.

The narrative suggests that Eun-Mi became consumed by a need for money and financial security. This obsession reportedly led her to create or participate in the "program" that Harvey is subjected to in the games—essentially monetizing her husband's suffering.

Whether Eun-Mi is truly villainous or simply a victim of circumstances herself is left somewhat ambiguous, adding moral complexity to the BLOODMONEY! universe.

Harvey Harvington Wife FAQ

Who is Harvey Harvington's wife?

Harvey Harvington's wife is Eun-Mi Harvington. She is also called Joy or Choi Eun-Mi, and she is a key character in BLOODMONEY family lore.

How old is Eun-Mi Harvington?

Eun-Mi is 34 years old, two years older than her husband Harvey (32). Her birthday is November 4th.

Is Eun-Mi playable in any game?

Yes! Eun-Mi is the playable protagonist in Human Expenditure Program (HEP), the sequel to BLOODMONEY! developed by SHROOMYCHRIST.

Why is Eun-Mi considered a villain?

The lore implies Eun-Mi created the "program" that traps Harvey. After Harvey lost his job, she developed an obsession with money. However, her true motivations remain somewhat ambiguous.

Does Eun-Mi Harvington appear in LoveMoney?

Eun-Mi does not appear directly in LoveMoney (the fan game by Buwu). However, she is referenced when Harvey expresses guilt about betraying his wife in the bad endings, so Eun-Mi remains important context even when she is off-screen.
