LoveMoney Android Compatibility: Huawei P30, Samsung & Mobile Play

We frequently see players asking if their specific Android devices—such as the widely used Huawei P30, Samsung Galaxy series, OnePlus, or Pixel phones—can run the LoveMoney 18+ visual novel. Because finding reliable information on indie game mobile compatibility is difficult, this guide will clarify exactly how Android users can access the game and what files to avoid.

Is There an Official LoveMoney APK for Android?

🚫 The Truth About Mobile Downloads

No official Android version exists. The sole developer, Buwu, has explicitly confirmed that they have never exported an official mobile APK for LoveMoney. The only supported platforms for downloadable clients are Windows PC and macOS.

Warning for Huawei P30 & Android Users:

If you see a website claiming to offer a "LoveMoney APK for Huawei P30" or asking you to bypass your device's security settings to install it, do not download it. These files are typically malicious scams designed to inject adware or steal data from Android devices.

How to Safely Play LoveMoney on Android (Huawei, Samsung, etc.)

You don't need to risk installing shady APKs just to play the game on your phone. Because LoveMoney utilizes simple clicker and dialogue mechanics, it ports incredibly well to mobile browsers.

Step-by-Step Mobile Web Guide:

  • 1
    Open your browser: Launch your default browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Huawei Browser) on your Android device.
  • 2
    Go to the play portal: Navigate to our Play LoveMoney Online section linked on our homepage.
