LoveMoney Game Plot Summary

Short version

The player needs a large amount of money for an urgent operation. Harvey Harvington becomes the key figure in how that money is earned. Each choice changes the emotional tone, Harvey's role, and the ending path.

LoveMoney takes the structure of a clicker and uses it as pressure. You are not clicking only for a score; you are trying to reach a medical survival goal while interacting with Harvey.

The story escalates because money, affection, guilt, and player agency overlap. The plot asks whether the player treats Harvey as a person, a resource, a romantic partner, or a means to an end.
